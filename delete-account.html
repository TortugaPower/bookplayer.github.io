Delete your BookPlayer account

This page explains how to request that your BookPlayer account and the data associated with it be deleted. BookPlayer is published by BookPlayer LLC.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app: open BookPlayer, go to the Profile tab, tap your account, and choose Delete account.
  2. By email: if you no longer have access to the app, send a request to support@bookplayer.app from the email address registered on your account. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your email address
  • Your BookPlayer user ID and authentication credentials (passkeys, sign-in tokens)
  • Your synced library metadata (books, folders, bookmarks, listening progress)
  • Custom artwork uploaded to BookPlayer's cloud sync
  • User preferences synced to your account

What data may be retained

  • Purchase and transaction records — retained by our payment processors (Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat) for the period legally required for tax and accounting purposes, typically up to 7 years in some jurisdictions. These records are not used to re-identify you in BookPlayer after account deletion.
  • Anonymized crash logs and diagnostic data — retained by our error reporting provider (Sentry) for up to 90 days for product reliability purposes. These logs do not include your email address or account identifiers.
  • Audiobook files stored locally on your device — these are not uploaded to BookPlayer's servers and are unaffected by account deletion. Uninstall the app or delete the files manually to remove them from your device.

Timeline

Deletion requests made through the app are typically completed within minutes. Requests submitted by email are completed within 30 days of verification.
