Chapter 1

Your listening experience

Made for books that last hours, not songs that last minutes.

  • Lock screen controls

    Play, pause and skip from the lock screen, control center or your headphones.

  • Chapters

    Play and jump between chapters, or back to the start of the book.

  • Playback speed

    Slow down a dense chapter or speed through the recap.

  • Smart rewind

    Goes back a little when you return after a longer pause, so you never lose the thread.

  • Volume boost

    Doubles the volume for quiet recordings and noisy commutes.

  • Sleep timer

    Stop after a set time or at the end of the chapter.

Support

Send us an email at support@bookplayer.app or open a new issue on GitHub.

Either way, please include the version of BookPlayer you're running (at the bottom of Settings), your device, and its system version.

We do our best to answer in time, but BookPlayer is a small team's labour of love, so please bear with us.
